Transparent by design

A compass with reasons.

The priority engine is deterministic and inspectable. AI can explain results later, but it should not invent the scoring truth.

01 / Score

Weighted, deterministic priority

Importance × weight
Urgency × weight
Impact × weight
Momentum × weight
Evidence confidence × weight

Each input is scored from 1 to 5. Each planning horizon changes the weights; scores are normalized to 100. Paused and completed goals are excluded from the recommendation.

02 / Recommend

Choose the highest eligible goal

The engine sorts eligible goals by score. A user-created “today priority” override wins until the end of the local day, and the interface labels it as a manual decision.

  • Show the score and input breakdown.
  • Explain deadline or blocker signals.
  • Keep user overrides distinguishable from calculated recommendations.
03 / Learn

Keep evidence quality intact

Activity is a raw event log. A repeated observation should only become a durable lesson after validation. Email-derived signals use transparent heuristics, save minimal metadata rather than full message bodies, and require your review.

Future integrations can read project/task/calendar sources and a bounded, provenance-aware Memory context. Missing or stale inputs should reduce confidence instead of being silently guessed.
04 / Current limits

What this version does not claim

  • It does not know your actual work unless you log it.
  • It does not read phone orientation sensors.
  • Only signed-in sessions sync private goals and activity to Supabase; local demo data remains browser-local.
  • Email reading requires a configured provider app and separate consent for each mailbox. Pandora Memory, calendar, finance, and phone sensors remain separate integrations.

The visual bearing compares the goal direction you selected with the direction recommended by the scoring engine. It is not GPS, a physical compass, or a measurement of your behavior.
